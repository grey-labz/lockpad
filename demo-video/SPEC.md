# Lockpad working prototype demo

GOAL: Show a locked-position owner the immediate funded-pool borrowing path and the marketplace alternative through one actual payout right.
OUTPUT: HyperFrames, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, local MP4 and editable project.
TIME: 120 seconds = 3,600 frames; the existing word-aligned narration sets the pace.
LOOK: Black #020202 canvas, evergreen #0d2818 surfaces, black-forest #04471c borders, sea-green #058c42 secondary state and malachite #16db65 actions. Neutral readable text complements the user's palette. Manrope 700, 64px chapters, 54px disclosure and proof notes. Chapters render at 12px and the disclosure at 10.125px at 360px playback width. Real app footage is uncropped at x=272/y=252, 1376×774. Header starts at x=192/y=108. No footage grade, grain, glow or vignette.
OBJECTS: Actual 1,000 TUSD payout NFT, 650 loan, 663 repayment, 920 sale, 1,000 maturity payout, original source timestamp and transaction receipts. No drawn app screens.
MOTION: Recorded cursor actions, direct workflow cuts and a 0.35-second registry-derived baseline draw under each chapter. Readable text holds. Footage compression is bounded at 1.6×; the maximum in this edit is 1.50×. Narration remains at 1×.
SOUND: Existing ElevenLabs Jack John – Conversational and Upbeat tracks. Voice-led walkthrough, without additional generation, music or synthetic effects.
