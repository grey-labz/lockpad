A locked position can still be valuable when you need liquidity today. Lockpad gives its owner two choices: borrow against it, or sell the payout right.

This prototype uses four NEAR contracts: a reference vault, a funded lending pool, a marketplace, and a test token. Alice owns a one thousand token position.

The pool can offer six hundred and fifty tokens now. With a one-time two percent fee, Alice would repay six hundred and sixty-three before the original maturity. These are illustrative prototype terms.

Alice accepts. Her payout right secures the loan, and six hundred and fifty test tokens reach her wallet. The principal stays in its source vault.

She repays six hundred and sixty-three tokens. The same payout NFT returns to her wallet, with its principal and original maturity preserved.

If the borrowing offer is too low, Alice can list instead. Here, she chooses nine hundred and twenty tokens. A buyer must accept that price.

Bob checks the position, reviews the price, and buys it. The original payout NFT reaches his wallet before the marketplace credits Alice's sale proceeds.

Alice withdraws her nine hundred and twenty tokens. The sale gives her liquidity while the underlying principal remains locked.

We advance local Sandbox time without changing the original maturity.

Bob then claims the full one thousand token payout from the reference vault. The app marks his position redeemed.

Each action leaves a receipt. This is proposed work before mainnet, using zero-value tokens. External DEX positions need a supported way to transfer or secure their payout rights.
