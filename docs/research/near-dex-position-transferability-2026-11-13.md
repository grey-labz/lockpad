# NEAR DEX positions for Lockpad

Research date: 13 November 2026. This is an integration shortlist, not a claim that Lockpad already supports these protocols.

**Start with Rhea Finance's DCL liquidity NFTs.** They provide the closest match to Lockpad's existing NFT custody model. Rhea classic LP shares and Jumbo classic LP shares are additional candidates, but need a separate multi-fungible-token adapter. Spin's option-vault shares are a conditional lead for positions with a native withdrawal delay. An ordinary transferable LP position is not automatically a transferable, time-locked position.

## Existing locks need different treatment

Rhea has a real LP/token locker at `token-locker.ref-labs.near`. Its source stores `locked_balance` and `unlock_time_sec` inside the depositor's account. `withdraw` checks the original account and refuses withdrawal before maturity. The retrieved deployed Wasm has inbound `ft_on_transfer` / `mft_on_transfer` and withdrawal methods, but **no NFT, account-claim transfer, or outgoing claim-assignment method**.

Consequently, a user who already locked LP shares there cannot simply transfer that locked claim to Lockpad. Wrapping a receipt after the fact would not give Lockpad control over the original withdrawal. The same restriction must be checked separately for farmed positions; the underlying LP asset's transfer API does not establish that a farming claim is transferable.

For a fixed-term product, the practical starting route is to deposit a transferable, unencumbered DCL NFT or LP shares into a Lockpad wrapper **before** the lock begins. The wrapper controls the position and issues an assignable release right with an immutable maturity. That release right can then secure a loan or be sold. Supporting a pre-existing non-transferable lock requires an approved beneficiary assignment or changes from the originating protocol.

Spin has a different model: its documented weekly withdrawal schedule may provide a native delay without a new LP locker. Vault shares have variable value and exposure to options settlement. A pending deposit or pending withdrawal can still be account-bound even if active shares are transferable; the adapter must distinguish those states. Current operational availability and redemption by the new share holder need to be proved.

## Product and lending implications

DEX LP positions are claims on changing token balances, rather than a fixed 1,000-token payout. Token prices, accumulated fees, range activity, and impermanent loss affect their value. The prototype's 65% borrowing limit and 2% one-time fee apply only to its fixed-payout reference instrument; they are not researched production terms for these DEX assets.

For Rhea DCL, the adapter must verify ownership with `nft_token` and inspect the position with `get_liquidity`. It must account for the two underlying assets, liquidity range, unclaimed fees, and farming status. A production pool also needs conservative valuation, a manipulation-resistant price source, liquidation rules, and payout recovery. A marketplace listing must accurately describe the position and the fees the buyer receives, without promising a fixed principal payout.

Rhea already offers LP-backed borrowing. Lockpad's proposed distinction is the choice between a borrowing offer and selling an eligible locked claim. Treat Rhea as an asset source and potential integration partner, rather than claiming that LP-backed borrowing itself is new.

## Prototype sequence

1. Use a Rhea DCL testnet liquidity NFT. The official contract list names `dclv2.ref-dev.testnet`; a read-only code query found a deployed contract there, with a different code hash from mainnet. Testnet compatibility must be checked rather than assumed.
2. Prove transfer into an isolated adapter, authoritative ownership after delivery, a rejected transfer returning to its owner, collateral return after repayment, sale delivery, fee handling, and redemption/removal by the new owner. A method exported in Wasm is not a completed transfer test.
3. Add an immutable-term wrapper if the asset needs to become a time-locked claim. Keep native LP value and any underlying fees explicit.
4. Add Rhea classic MFT support, then assess Jumbo's classic pools with the same custody and registration tests.
5. Seek protocol confirmation for already farmed or locked claims before enabling them. No outreach or public transactions were performed for this research.
6. Investigate Spin's active vault-share MFT IDs, epoch progression, transfer acceptance, and recipient withdrawal as a separate delayed-withdrawal integration. Do not assume its option tokens, active vault shares, pending deposits, and pending withdrawals all carry the same rights.
